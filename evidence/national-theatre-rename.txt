Target feature: national-arts-theatre-iganmu
Location: Iganmu, Lagos

Current recorded name: National Arts Theatre
Proposed name: Wole Soyinka Centre for Culture and the Creative Arts

The official National Theatre Our Story source states that the fully renovated complex was officially commissioned and re-dedicated on 1 October 2025 as the Wole Soyinka Centre for Culture and the Creative Arts. It also identifies the National Theatre as a cultural landmark located at Iganmu, Lagos.

The official National Theatre Wole Soyinka Centre source states that the edifice was formerly known as the National Theatre and was renamed the Wole Soyinka Centre in honour of Wole Soyinka.
